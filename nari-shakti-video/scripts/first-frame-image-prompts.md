# First-Frame Image Prompts -- All 7 Parts
> **Workflow:** Generate First-Frame Still -> Upload to Veo3 as starting frame -> Veo3 animates the clip
> **Tool:** Any image gen tool -- Flux, Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Firefly, etc.
> **No reference images needed** -- all characters and settings are described inside each prompt.
> **Orientation:** Landscape 16:9 (all prompts include this)

---

> **How to use:**
> 1. Copy-paste the prompt exactly
> 2. Generate the image
> 3. Save it with the filename shown
> 4. Upload to Veo3 as the **starting frame** for that part

---

## PART 1 -- First Frame
**Scene:** India's Parliament building at sunrise -- majestic, hopeful opening

```
2.5D animated cartoon still frame, landscape 16:9. India's new Sansad Bhavan Parliament
building -- distinctive triangular/pyramidal stone roof, grand entrance staircase, tall
ornate pillars, circular stone facade. Camera slightly low angle, wide shot looking upward.
Sky behind the building is a deep warm golden-orange sunrise -- saffron and gold rays burst
from behind the triangular roof dramatically, like a crown of light. The building looks
powerful, historic, and majestic. No people or characters in the frame.
Style: 2.5D animated cartoon illustration, motion-graphic, warm golden color grade,
cinematic wide angle, Indian political documentary feel.
```

## PART 2 -- First Frame

```
2.5D animated cartoon still frame, landscape 16:9. Split-screen composition, clean vertical
divider at the center of the frame.

LEFT HALF -- Village home interior: A woman in her 30s, wearing a bright red-orange cotton

through a glass window behind her.

Both women have expressions of quiet, trembling hope. Soft golden-saffron bokeh particles
float in the air of both panels. Warm golden-orange color palette throughout. Wide shot --
full body of both women visible.
Style: 2.5D animated cartoon illustration, warm glow, split-screen graphic design.
```

## PART 3 -- First Frame
**Scene:** Parliament interior goes suddenly cold and dark -- opposition figures stir

```
2.5D animated cartoon still frame, landscape 16:9. Interior of India's Parliament chamber --
wide shot from the back of the chamber looking toward the central podium. The chamber has
rows of curved wooden benches on both sides, a high domed ceiling with intricate architecture,
a large central podium/speaker's table at the front.

BUT the entire scene is now in cold dark grey and icy blue -- all golden warmth is completely
gone, replaced by a harsh, ominous cold atmosphere. On the opposition benches: dark silhouette
figures (no faces -- solid dark shadows in the shape of seated people, some in white kurtas)
are beginning to stir, turning toward each other, whispering. At the center speaker's table:
a glowing paper document labeled "Nari Shakti Vandana Adhiniyam" sits with an unstable,
flickering deep red glow -- like it is under threat.

Atmosphere is tense, ominous, heavy. Cold blue-grey light casts long shadows.
Style: 2.5D animated cartoon illustration, cold dark color grade, ominous mood, wide angle.
```

## PART 6 -- First Frame
**Scene:** Three women stand side by side, facing camera, demanding answers -- fierce and angry

```
2.5D animated cartoon still frame, landscape 16:9. Three Indian women standing side by side,
centered in frame, all three facing directly at the camera with fierce, angry, righteous
expressions. Bold deep red and black background -- solid color, no location, pulsing crimson
energy radiating behind them.

LEFT WOMAN (rural): A woman in her mid-30s, bright red-orange cotton saree, red sindoor on
forehead, round bindi, dark hair in bun, warm brown skin, simple gold earrings. Expression:
fierce and furious, jaw set, eyes burning with anger. Arms at sides, fists slightly clenched.

CENTER WOMAN (urban): A young woman in her mid-20s, light-blue salwar-kameez, hair open and
straight, sharp confident face. Expression: jaw clenched, eyes blazing with righteous anger,
pointing one finger directly at the viewer accusingly.

RIGHT WOMAN (elderly): An older woman in her 60s, pure white saree with a thin gold border,
silver-white hair in a neat bun, deep wise eyes, dignified posture. Expression: fierce and
dignified -- the anger of someone who has waited decades and will not wait anymore.

All three in full-body shot, equal size. The background pulses deep red -- intense,
graphic, protest-poster energy.
Style: 2.5D animated cartoon illustration, bold graphic composition, deep red color grade.
```

## PART 7 -- First Frame
**Scene:** Thousands of women marching together -- Parliament building visible at the horizon

```
2.5D animated cartoon still frame, landscape 16:9. Epic wide shot of a massive crowd of
Indian women marching forward, filling the entire frame from left edge to right edge and
from the middle ground all the way to the horizon.

FRONT ROW (largest, most detailed, clearly lit):
- LEFT: A woman in her mid-30s -- bright red-orange cotton saree, red sindoor on forehead,
  dark hair in bun, warm brown skin, gold earrings. Expression: fierce and determined,
  arm raised and pointing forward.
- CENTER: A young woman in her mid-20s -- light-blue salwar-kameez, hair open, sharp eyes.
  Expression: resolute and powerful, marching forward with purpose, chin raised.
- RIGHT: An older woman in her 60s -- pure white saree with thin gold border, silver-white
  hair in bun, wise fierce eyes. Expression: unstoppable and dignified, one arm raised high.

BEHIND THEM (mid-ground to horizon): Hundreds of Indian women of all ages -- sarees in every
color, salwars, kurtas, headscarves -- a sea of color stretching to infinity. Every woman
is marching forward, many with raised fists or pointing arms.

SKY: Dark night sky with a faint warm glow on the horizon.

DISTANT BACKGROUND (at the horizon): India's Sansad Bhavan Parliament building --
triangular/pyramidal roof, grand pillars -- faintly visible and warmly lit in gold,
the destination of this march.

The crowd feels infinite and unstoppable. Vibrant warm colors of the crowd pop against
the dark sky.
Style: 2.5D animated cartoon illustration, epic wide panoramic composition, night sky,
warm crowd colors, cinematic scale.
```
