## PART 1 -- First Frame
**Scene:** Parliament chamber interior — cartoon PM figure (grey beard, white kurta, saffron shawl) at podium presenting glowing bill

```
2.5D animated cartoon still frame, landscape 16:9. Interior of India's new Parliament
chamber (Sansad Bhavan) -- wide shot from audience side looking toward the central
speaker's podium. Grand circular chamber with tiered wooden benches filled with seated
cartoon figures, high ornate domed ceiling, warm golden-saffron light flooding in
dramatically from above, like a sunrise inside the hall.

AT THE CENTRAL PODIUM: A tall dignified cartoon figure stands upright and commanding.
He wears a pristine white kurta-pajama with a bright saffron/orange shawl draped over
his left shoulder. He has a full, neatly trimmed grey beard, neat white-grey hair, and
deep confident eyes. Both his hands are raised, holding up a glowing official document.
The document emits warm golden-white light as if it holds great historic importance.
The text on the document reads: नारी शक्ति वंदन अधिनियम. A warm golden-saffron aura
radiates around this figure -- he is the hero of this moment.

The seated cartoon figures on the benches face him with attention. The entire chamber
glows with warm sunrise-like golden light. Golden bokeh particles float gently upward
in the air.

Style: 2.5D animated cartoon illustration, NOT photorealistic, cartoon proportions
and outlines must be clear, warm golden saffron color grade, cinematic Parliament
interior, Indian political documentary feel.
```

## PART 3 -- First Frame
**Scene:** Parliament interior goes suddenly cold and dark -- opposition figures stir

```
2.5D animated cartoon still frame, landscape 16:9. Interior of India's Parliament chamber --
wide shot from the back of the chamber looking toward the central podium. The chamber has
rows of curved wooden benches on both sides, a high domed ceiling with intricate architecture,
a large central podium/speaker's table at the front.

BUT the entire scene is now in cold dark grey and icy blue -- all golden warmth is completely
gone, replaced by a harsh, ominous cold atmosphere. On the opposition benches: dark silhouette
figures (no faces -- solid dark shadows in the shape of seated people, some in white kurtas)
are beginning to stir, turning toward each other, whispering. At the center speaker's table:
a glowing paper document labeled "Nari Shakti Vandana Adhiniyam" sits with an unstable,
flickering deep red glow -- like it is under threat.

Atmosphere is tense, ominous, heavy. Cold blue-grey light casts long shadows.
Style: 2.5D animated cartoon illustration, cold dark color grade, ominous mood, wide angle.
```

## PART 7 -- First Frame
**Scene:** Thousands of women marching together -- Parliament building visible at the horizon

```
2.5D animated cartoon still frame, landscape 16:9. Epic wide shot of a massive crowd of
Indian women marching forward, filling the entire frame from left edge to right edge and
from the middle ground all the way to the horizon.

FRONT ROW (largest, most detailed, clearly lit):
- LEFT: A woman in her mid-30s -- bright red-orange cotton saree, red sindoor on forehead,
  dark hair in bun, warm brown skin, gold earrings. Expression: fierce and determined,
  arm raised and pointing forward.
- CENTER: A young woman in her mid-20s -- light-blue salwar-kameez, hair open, sharp eyes.
  Expression: resolute and powerful, marching forward with purpose, chin raised.
- RIGHT: An older woman in her 60s -- pure white saree with thin gold border, silver-white
  hair in bun, wise fierce eyes. Expression: unstoppable and dignified, one arm raised high.

BEHIND THEM (mid-ground to horizon): Hundreds of Indian women of all ages -- sarees in every
color, salwars, kurtas, headscarves -- a sea of color stretching to infinity. Every woman
is marching forward, many with raised fists or pointing arms.

SKY: Dark night sky with a faint warm glow on the horizon.

DISTANT BACKGROUND (at the horizon): India's Sansad Bhavan Parliament building --
triangular/pyramidal roof, grand pillars -- faintly visible and warmly lit in gold,
the destination of this march.

The crowd feels infinite and unstoppable. Vibrant warm colors of the crowd pop against
the dark sky.
Style: 2.5D animated cartoon illustration, epic wide panoramic composition, night sky,
warm crowd colors, cinematic scale.
```
