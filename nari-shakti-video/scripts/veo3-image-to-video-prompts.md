## PART 1 — "नई उम्मीद की भोर" (0:00–0:08)

**Starting Image to upload:** `frames/part-01-first-frame.png`
*(Parliament interior — cartoon PM figure at podium holding glowing bill)*

```
Animate this cartoon Parliament chamber scene into a living 8-second cinematic clip.
2.5D animated cartoon style throughout — do NOT make it photorealistic. Cartoon outlines
and proportions must be preserved.

PM FIGURE ANIMATION (white kurta, saffron shawl, full grey beard):
- Natural dignified breathing — his chest and shoulders rise and fall slowly, posture
  remains upright and commanding throughout
- The glowing document he holds pulses warmly — golden light breathes outward in slow
  gentle waves, like a heartbeat of historic importance
- His eyes are steady, wise, confident — natural blink timing (every 3-4 seconds)
  looking out over the chamber with quiet pride
- His saffron shawl sways very gently with his breathing — cloth physics, subtle drape
  movement, not stiff
- His hands holding the document have slight weight and solidity — the document is
  held firmly, presented to the chamber

CHAMBER ENVIRONMENT ANIMATION:
- The warm golden-saffron light fills the chamber like a sunrise — the light breathes
  softly, expanding and relaxing gently
- Seated cartoon figures on the benches are visible — subtle motion: slight head turns,
  gentle shifting, conveying a rapt chamber audience
- Golden bokeh particles float slowly upward throughout the chamber air
- The high domed ceiling has warm golden light moving gently across it

CAMERA: slow cinematic push-in from wide chamber shot → gradually moving toward the
podium — the PM figure grows larger and more prominent. By the end of 8 seconds, he
and the glowing document fill the center of the frame, dominating the composition.

Style: 2.5D animated cartoon, warm golden-saffron, cinematic, NOT photorealistic.
Duration: 8 seconds.
```

## PART 2 — "बहनों की चमक" (0:08–0:18)

**Starting Image to upload:** `frames/part-02-first-frame.png`  
*(Split-screen — rural woman left, urban woman right, both watching news)*

```
Animate this split-screen cartoon illustration into a living 10-second scene.
The 2.5D cartoon illustration style must be preserved — lifelike motion only, not
photorealistic rendering.

LEFT PANEL — Rural woman in village home:
- She is seated cross-legged, watching a small TV that glows with warm light
- Natural realistic breathing — her chest and shoulders rise and fall gently
- Her eyes blink naturally (every 3–4 seconds, human blink timing)
- Her expression shifts slowly from neutral → trembling hope — eyebrows lift
  slightly, lips part softly, the corners of her eyes glisten with unshed tears
- Her saree fabric has gentle cloth physics — the drape over her shoulder sways
  slightly as she breathes
- The brass diya flame flickers realistically in the corner — warm dancing light
  casts animated shadows on the mud wall behind her
- Golden bokeh particles rise gently from the diya flame into the air

RIGHT PANEL — Urban woman in city apartment:
- She is standing holding a smartphone, reading a news headline
- Natural breathing animation — shoulders and chest move
- Her eyes blink naturally; she reads the phone screen — pupils shift slightly as
  she scans the text
- Expression: quiet disbelief turning to slow radiant smile — the smile builds
  gradually over 4–5 seconds, mouth curves, cheeks lift
- Her hair moves very subtly in a light indoor air current — not dramatic, just
  alive
- The phone screen glows and reflects a faint light on her face — light source
  interaction, realistic but cartoon
- City skyline visible through window behind her — window glass has a subtle
  gleam, city lights twinkle softly

Both panels:
- Golden-saffron bokeh particles float gently in both spaces
- Warm golden light fills both panels — matching glow, unified feeling
- Vertical divider between panels is clean and sharp

Camera: static (no camera movement) — the emotion carries the motion.
Duration: 10 seconds.
```

## PART 3 — "अचानक रुका सफर" (0:18–0:26)

**Starting Image to upload:** `frames/part-03-first-frame.png`  
*(Parliament interior, cold grey, shadow figures stirring, bill document flickering)*

```
Animate this cartoon Parliament interior from a state of eerie stillness into building
chaos — 8 seconds. 2.5D animated cartoon style throughout, NO photorealism.

Environment animation:
- The entire chamber is bathed in cold grey-blue light — this light has a slow,
  ominous pulse, like a heartbeat: slightly dimmer → slightly brighter → repeat
- The high domed ceiling has faint cold shadows moving across it — as if storm
  clouds were passing outside the skylights
- Dust motes float in the cold air — slow, heavy, oppressive

Shadow figures on benches (dark cartoon silhouettes, no faces):
- Begin completely still — then one by one they start to move
- First one turns its head to whisper to another (side profile turn, natural neck
  rotation)
- Two figures lean toward each other — conspiratorial, hunched shoulders
- Arms begin to cross, papers are picked up and shuffled — hands and paper move
  with natural weight and physics
- By the end of the clip: all 4–5 shadow figures are alert, turned toward center,
  clearly plotting — building tension

Glowing bill document on the speaker's table:
- Its warm golden glow flickers unstably — like a candle in wind
- Every 2 seconds the glow dims sharply then recovers — visually suggests threat
- The document trembles slightly, paper edges vibrate faintly

Camera: begins on a wide shot → very slowly pushes in toward the opposition benches,
increasing dread. Cold blue light intensifies as camera advances.
Duration: 8 seconds.
```

## PART 4 — "विश्वासघात का चेहरा" (0:26–0:34)

**Starting Image to upload:** `frames/part-04-first-frame.png`  
*(Parliament corridor, white-kurta figure seen from BEHIND, arms beginning to rise)*

```
Animate this cartoon corridor scene — the opposition figure walks away from camera
and out through the Parliament exit. 8 seconds. 2.5D animated cartoon style, NO
photorealism. CRITICAL: the figure's face must NEVER be shown — back-view ONLY
throughout the entire clip.

Character animation — white kurta figure (back view only):
- At clip start he is mid-stride, already facing away
- Natural realistic walk cycle — weight shift side to side, arms swing naturally
  with walking rhythm, legs extend and plant with gravity
- His kurta fabric moves with body physics — the hemline sways, the back fabric
  pulls and releases with each step — silk/cotton cloth simulation feel
- His dark hair sways slightly with his movement
- His arms gradually rise as he walks — starting from sides → slowly spreading
  outward like a dramatic walking-away exit
- He slows as he reaches the exit archway — pauses for ONE beat (deliberate,
  theatrical) — then steps through and disappears into darkness beyond

Shadow and lighting:
- His long cartoon shadow stretches dramatically behind him along the stone floor
  — the shadow moves with him in sync, slightly exaggerated in length
- As he moves forward, cold blue light from ahead of him grows brighter — light
  source at the exit floods him from the front (silhouetting him from behind)
- The chamber doorway behind him (in background) glows faintly cold blue —
  implying the chaos he has left behind

Sound design note: footsteps echo in the corridor — clip timing should feel like
each step has weight.

Camera: starts behind and slightly above → very slowly pushes forward following
him → as he exits, camera slows to a stop at the threshold. Cold, cinematic.
Duration: 8 seconds.
```

## PART 5 — "गिरता हुआ सपना" (0:34–0:42)

**Starting Image to upload:** `frames/part-05-first-frame.png`  
*(Abstract split space — bill document floating, tilted toward dark side)*

```
Animate this abstract conceptual scene — the bill document struggles between light
and dark forces and begins to fall. 8 seconds. 2.5D animated cartoon style.

Background space:
- RIGHT half (warm golden light): slow breathing pulse — the golden light expands
  and contracts subtly like a living heartbeat. Warm bokeh particles drift upward.
- LEFT half (cold dark shadow): slow churning darkness — the grey shadow roils like
  slow dark smoke. A few cold icy particles drift downward.
- The dividing line between the two halves shivers and warps slightly — as if the
  dark side is pushing against the boundary, trying to expand

Bill document (main subject — floating in upper center):
- At start: floating still, tilted slightly left, golden glow around it
- Over 8 seconds, the document slowly tilts further left — gravity of the dark
  side pulling it
- The document's golden glow flickers and stutters — like power going out
- Paper edges flutter with natural paper physics — slight warp and curl
- As it tilts, more of the document crosses into the dark half of the frame
  — the portions in the dark lose their golden glow instantly
- By end: the document is now at 45 degrees left tilt, nearly half in darkness,
  glow barely holding — frozen at the brink of falling completely

No human characters. The document is the only animated subject.
Camera: completely static — the document's movement IS the drama.
Duration: 8 seconds.
```

## PART 6 — "जवाब दो" (0:42–0:52)

**Starting Image to upload:** `frames/part-06-first-frame.png`  
*(Three women side by side, deep red background, fierce expressions)*

```
Animate this three-women confrontation scene — they are furious, righteous, and
demanding. The most emotionally intense clip of the series. 10 seconds.
2.5D animated cartoon illustration style — but faces must have near-realistic
micro-expression quality: the rage and pain must FEEL real.

ALL THREE WOMEN — shared animation:
- Natural breathing — but heavy, angry breathing: chests rise sharply with each
  breath, jaw muscles are tight, nostrils flare subtly with each exhale
- Eyes: intense, direct contact with camera. Pupils are steady — the kind of
  focused rage that does not look away. Blink rate is reduced (anger reduces
  blinking — natural human micro-behavior)
- Hair and clothing move in a slow dramatic energy wind — not a real wind but an
  emotional one: the deep red background pulses and the energy lifts hair edges,
  saree borders, dupatta — a subtle cinematic effect

LEFT WOMAN (rural, red-orange saree, sindoor):
- Her fists are clenched at her sides — she unclenches and re-clenches them twice
  during the clip — restraining herself
- Her jaw is set hard; she swallows once — real throat movement
- Her bindi and sindoor glow slightly in the pulsing red light
- Near the end: her arm begins to rise slowly from her side — fist forming,
  about to point forward

CENTER WOMAN (urban, light-blue salwar, open hair):
- She is already pointing one finger at the camera — hold that pose, but make
  the arm feel weighted and angry, not stiff
- Her finger trembles very slightly — controlled rage
- Her lips move once — she mouths a silent word (as if about to speak)
- Her hair gets a subtle energy-wind lift near the end of the clip

RIGHT WOMAN (elderly, white saree, silver bun):
- She is the most still — her stillness is more terrifying than the others
- Slow, deliberate breathing — each breath is visible
- Her wise eyes carry decades of pain — the deepest emotion in her face
- One single tear forms at the corner of one eye during the clip — does NOT
  fall — just glistens and holds

Background:
- Deep red pulsing — like a heartbeat, matching the anger rhythm
- Occasional crackle of energy: spark-like micro-flashes in the background, very
  brief — like electricity in the air
- The pulsing red casts dramatic upward shadows on their faces — cinematic, bold

Camera: starts wide showing all three → slow push-in over 10 seconds to a
medium-close 3-shot. By the last second, all three faces fill the frame.
Duration: 10 seconds.
```

## PART 7 — "देश की बेटियाँ" (0:52–1:05)

**Starting Image to upload:** `frames/part-07-first-frame.png`  
*(Epic wide shot — thousands of women marching, Parliament on horizon, night sky)*

```
Animate this epic crowd march scene — the most powerful and hopeful moment of the
series. 13 seconds. 2.5D animated cartoon illustration style — sweeping,
cinematic, emotionally overwhelming.

CROWD ANIMATION (mid-ground to horizon):
- The entire crowd is in motion — a living, breathing river of women marching
  forward toward camera and toward the Parliament building
- Crowd wave motion: rows of cartoon women sway gently in a synchronized ripple
  effect (like a real crowd photographed from above — natural undulation, not robotic)
- Raised fists bob with march rhythm — staggered timing, not all synchronized
- Sarees and dupattas in every color flutter and flow — cloth physics, wind direction
  is forward (toward camera), adding to the unstoppable forward energy
- The crowd recedes to the horizon with correct perspective scaling

FRONT ROW — THREE WOMEN (most detailed animation):

  LEFT (rural woman, red-orange saree):
  - She marches forward with powerful, grounded steps — each step has weight
  - Her arm is raised and pointing forward — she holds the pose but the arm
    has living weight, slight tremor of determination
  - Her saree pallu flies behind her in the forward wind
  - Her expression: fierce, tearful joy — eyes wet but jaw set, this is victory

  CENTER (urban woman, light-blue salwar):
  - She marches with her chin raised high — confident, purposeful stride
  - Her open hair streams behind her in the wind
  - Her eyes are forward, unwavering — she looks directly at the Parliament horizon

  RIGHT (elderly woman, white saree):
  - She marches too — slower, dignified steps, but her back is straight
  - Her raised arm is steady — she will not lower it
  - Her white saree catches the warm gold Parliament light from the horizon

SKY AND ATMOSPHERE:
- Dark night sky transitions slowly to having a warm amber glow on the horizon —
  the Parliament building is lit warmly, like a beacon
- Stars in the sky (subtle cartoon stars, not photorealistic) twinkle gently
- Parliament building on the horizon: its lights glow warm gold, windows lit up —
  it grows very slightly larger as the crowd approaches (the march IS progress)

LIGHT EFFECT:
- The warm golden light from the Parliament horizon falls on the front-row women —
  they are bathed in hope-light while the background is still in darkness
- This light seems to reach OUT toward the marching women

Camera: starts at ground level in front of the crowd — slightly low angle, making
the crowd look enormous and unstoppable. VERY slow dolly back (camera retreats
slowly) as the crowd marches toward it — this makes the crowd feel like it is
endlessly advancing. Camera also tilts up slightly over 13 seconds to reveal more
of the Parliament building and sky.

Final second: camera holds — Parliament glows, crowd fills the frame edge to edge.
Duration: 13 seconds.
```

## Motion Quality Tips — "Real type" cartoon animation ke liye

Veo3 se best result ke liye yeh words zaroor add karo har prompt ke saath:

```
natural breathing animation, cloth physics simulation, realistic eye blink timing,
weight-based walk cycle, cinematic camera motion, micro-expressions,
2.5D animated cartoon style, NOT photorealistic
```

Agar Veo3 photorealistic render karne lage — starting image dobara upload karo
aur `cartoon illustration only, preserve 2D art style, no 3D rendering` explicitly add karo.
